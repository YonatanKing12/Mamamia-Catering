# מה צריך מהדוד

כל פריט כאן הוא עובדה עסקית שאסור לנחש. אוחד מ-14 סוכני מחקר.

1. Kashrut status per branch — certified or not, and by which body (rabbanut / בד"ץ / none). This is a strategy-blocking fact: it determines whether the אזכרה / ברית / שבת חתן / בר מצווה occasion pages can be built at all, and whether a kashrut trust badge exists to display. Must never be guessed.
2. Minimum order per format (מגשי אירוח / בופה / מוגש בצלחות / עם מלצרים) — in portions and/or ₪. Competitors publish 12 units, 25 portions, 20 diners, 8 dishes.
3. Lead time per format — hours or days of advance notice, and whether same-day or next-day is possible. Competitors publish 24 hours to 3–4 days.
4. Real starting price per person per format, AND the itemised list of what is included (disposables, waiters, delivery, setup, teardown, VAT). If no number can be given, the inclusions list ships alone — no invented anchor.
5. Delivery radius per branch (which cities/neighbourhoods each kitchen serves), delivery fee, and delivery time windows / slot length.
6. Maximum production capacity per kitchen — how many diners each of the three kitchens can produce for in a day, and any day-of-week constraints.
7. Whether self-pickup is offered at each branch, its minimum (if any), and pickup hours.
8. Names, roles, and tenure of the standing kitchen team per branch (chef names at minimum) — required to break the category's anonymity.
9. Exact branch addresses, phone numbers, and opening hours for Herzliya Pituach, Raanana, Petah Tikva.
10. Which restaurant menu items are actually available for catering — needed to build the "taste it tonight in the restaurant" bridge and to cross-link catering dishes to live menu dishes.
11. Real photography from the three restaurants and real catering jobs (kitchens, teams, plated food, actual events) — the authentic visual direction is not deliverable on stock imagery.
12. Named catering clients willing to be credited, and real testimonials with documented consent. Absent these, the site ships with zero testimonials rather than placeholders.
13. Whether on-site cooking is offered (pasta station, pizza oven, chef on site) — competitors PizzaParty and המנגליסטים own the live-station angle and this is a plausible Mamamia strength, but only if true.
14. Business/procurement terms for corporate leads: invoice and payment terms, ability to be onboarded as an approved supplier (ספק מאושר), recurring-order handling, and dietary coverage (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
15. Existing Google Business profile URLs and current review counts per branch, plus any rest.co.il / easy.co.il / mit4mit listings — real, reusable social proof.
